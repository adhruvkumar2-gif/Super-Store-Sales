#Store sales dashboard 

📌 Project Overview

This project represents a Sales Dashboard Analytics System developed using Power BI to analyze retail sales performance across different dimensions such as category, region, customer segment, and time.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive visual analysis of sales, profit, and operational metrics, enabling business stakeholders to monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data visualization, KPI tracking, and business intelligence reporting, which are essential for Data Analyst and BI roles.

📁 Dataset

The dataset used in this dashboard includes retail sales transaction data with the following components:

✔ Order details (Order Date, Sales Amount, Profit)
✔ Product information (Category, Sub-category)
✔ Customer segmentation (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
✔ Shipping details (Ship Mode)
✔ Payment modes (COD, Online, Cards)
✔ Regional and state-level data

The dataset is structured to support time-series analysis, segmentation, and performance tracking.

🛠 Tools & Technologies

Purpose-wise tools used in the project:

Data Visualization: Power BI
Data Processing: Power BI Query Editor
Data Modeling: Relationships & Measures in Power BI
🧠 Workflow / Steps
🔹 Data Loading
Imported dataset into Power BI
Verified column types and relationships
Prepared data model for analysis
🔹 Data Transformation
Cleaned and formatted fields using Power Query
Ensured consistency in date formats and categorical variables
Created calculated columns and measures
🔹 Data Modeling
Established relationships between tables (if multiple tables exist)
Created DAX measures for KPIs such as Sales, Profit, and Quantity
🔹 Dashboard Development

The Power BI dashboard was designed to present insights in a clear and interactive manner, including:

✔ KPI Cards
✔ Category-wise and Sub-category analysis
✔ Time-series trends
✔ Regional performance
✔ Customer segmentation
✔ Operational metrics

📊 Dashboard Components
✔ KPI Summary
Total Sales: 2M
Total Profit: 175K
Total Quantity: 22K
Average Shipping Time: 3.93 days

✔ Sales by Category
Office Supplies lead in sales
Furniture and Technology follow

✔ Sales by Sub-Category
Tables generate the highest sales
Bookcases and Copiers contribute moderately

✔ Sales by Ship Mode
Standard Class is the most used shipping method
First Class, Second Class, and Same Day follow

✔ Monthly Sales & Profit (YoY)
Sales and profit trends are visualized across months
Comparison between years (2019 and 2020) shows growth patterns

✔ Regional Analysis
West region contributes the highest share (~32%)
East (~29%) and South (~16%) follow

✔ Customer Segmentation
Consumer segment dominates (~30%+)
Corporate and Home Office contribute smaller shares

✔ Payment Mode Distribution
COD: ~42%
Online: ~37%
Cards: ~22%

✔ Time-Series Analysis
Sales trends plotted over time (2019–2021)
Identifies seasonal peaks and dips

✔ Geographical Insights
State-wise sales and profit distribution
Helps identify high-performing and low-performing regions

📌 Key Results & Insights

📈 Strong Sales Performance:
The business achieved total sales of 2M with steady growth over time.

💰 Profitability:
Total profit stands at 175K, indicating moderate profit margins.

🛍 Category Insights:
Office Supplies dominate in volume, while other categories contribute strategically.

🌍 Regional Trends:
West region is the top-performing region, followed by East.

🚚 Operational Efficiency:
Standard shipping is the most preferred due to cost-effectiveness.

💳 Customer Payment Behavior:
COD is the most used payment method, showing trust and accessibility preferences.

📊 Seasonality:
Sales and profit show fluctuations across months, indicating seasonal demand patterns.

🎯 Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard successfully transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations. It enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs, understand customer behavior, and evaluate operational efficiency.

This project highlights the importance of data visualization in decision-making and demonstrates strong BI and analytical capabilities.
